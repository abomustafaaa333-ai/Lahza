# Product reference for Lahza demo catalog

## UI reference supplied by user

The target mobile pattern uses an RTL, image-first category grid followed by product offer cards. Each product card has a large product image and a circular add button. Lahza will preserve its own burgundy, coral, and cream identity while using this layout principle.

## Public market references consulted

- Syria Market: https://syriaamarket.com/
  - Relevant product families: cheeses and dairy, oils and ghee, ready-to-cook foods, cold cuts, kibbeh and sambousek, sweets, coffee and roasts, nuts, olives and pickles, salads and appetizers, dried fruit, bakery, zaatar and legumes, snacks, spices, honey, dates, ice cream, and homemade foods.
  - Examples: halloumi, makdous, labneh, shanklish, manakish, olives, hummus, tahini, coffee, spices, nuts, and pickles.
- Damascus Gates: https://damascusgates.com/
  - Relevant Syrian market range: dairy, oils, legumes, grains, nuts, chocolate, ice cream, spices, and traditional Syrian foods.

## Implementation decision

The demo catalog will use broad Syrian-market product families from these references. Product names, images, availability, and prices remain editable later from the owner dashboard. Prices will start as confirmation-required placeholders rather than claiming current market prices, which vary significantly.
